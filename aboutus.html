About Me

My name is Victor Lanky, the founder and CEO of Esebagbon Automobiles. I am passionate about helping people find vehicles that match their lifestyle, ambitions and budget. My experience in vehicle sourcing, customer service and digital communication has shaped a dealership built on trust, clear information and dependable support.

Esebagbon Automobiles brings together premium cars, capable SUVs, family vehicles and performance models for customers in Nigeria and beyond. I believe that buying a car should feel exciting and secure, so I focus on honest descriptions, thoughtful recommendations and after-sales care. My long-term goal is to grow a respected automotive brand that makes quality vehicles accessible while delivering a truly personal buying experience.
